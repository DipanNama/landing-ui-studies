# Interface Foundations - reference notes

Source observed: https://uidesigncourse.com/ (landing page only), 19 September 2026.

## Observed system

- White canvas with a faint 24px dot grid in the hero.
- Black ink, cool gray supporting copy, and one vivid blue accent.
- Hero headline at desktop: roughly 56px, weight 600, 1.05 line-height, slightly tight tracking; centered and capped near 720px.
- Compact 16px body copy, 1.55 line-height, capped near 72 characters.
- Capsule CTA with high-radius black fill; monospaced uppercase sale label.
- Hero includes measurement annotations and selection-box styling, turning the page into a live design lesson.
- Content uses a narrow reading column, thin rules, restrained spacing, and very little decoration.
- Sections: expectations, expandable curriculum, prompt/code example, audience groups, final offer.
- Mobile keeps the top actions visible, drops annotations, reduces headline size to about 36px, and stacks content.

## Original substitutions in this study
