# Quiet App Download Landing

Use this skill for a focused desktop-tool landing page where the product can be explained in one sentence and shown in one strong interface image.

## Core recipe

1. Use a white or near-white canvas with almost no decoration.
2. Keep the header 64-76px high. Place a compact app mark left and one low-priority link right.
3. Build a vertically centered hero with generous top space: large app mark, 32-40px semibold title, short 1-2 line description, black primary CTA, then a muted compatibility note.
4. Let one product window carry the proof. Make it wide, lightly bordered, softly shadowed, and rounded only where a native desktop window would be.
5. Keep the footer tiny and neutral. Do not add marketing sections merely to fill space.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#ffffff`
- Primary text: `#090909`
- Secondary text: `#777777`
- Hairline: `#e7e7e7`
- CTA: black with white text
- Accent: one cool blue used sparingly inside the product mark or mockup
- Typeface: tight display sans with system fallback
- Radius: 8-12px controls; 18-24px large app mark
- Shadow: broad and very soft, around `0 0 28px rgba(0,0,0,.10)`

## Responsive rules

- Hold 32px mobile side gutters.
- Keep the header height unchanged.
- Allow the headline to wrap naturally to two or three centered lines.
- Keep the CTA tap target at least 44px tall.
- Scale the product window as one coherent object. Simplify its internal type and spacing instead of rearranging every pane.
- Preserve a glimpse of the interface near the first mobile fold.

## Avoid

- Multi-color gradients across the page.
- Decorative cards before the product proof.
- Long navigation, badges, social proof rows, pricing grids, or FAQ sections.
- Copying a real app's branding, screenshots, names, release files, or author identity.
