Source observed: https://promptbuilder.site/ on September 19, 2026 (IST).

## Page structure

- Very restrained white landing page with a 72px header.
- Small square product mark at upper left and a single text link at upper right.
- Centered hero: oversized app mark, one compact headline, a two-line explanatory paragraph, one black download button, and a quiet platform note.
- One large product screenshot is the main proof element. It sits in a soft shadow, has rounded top corners, and extends toward the fold.
- Minimal footer with copyright at left and two links at right.

## Visual measurements and behavior

- Desktop reference captured at 1440 × 1000; full page height was about 1,949px.
- Mobile reference captured at 390 × 844; full page height was about 1,079px.
- Header height: 72px on mobile and desktop.
- Desktop H1: 36px, weight 600, tight display sans. Mobile title grows visually through narrower wrapping into two centered lines.
- White background, nearly black text, neutral gray body copy, black CTA with a 12px radius.
- Desktop hero screenshot is broad and dominant. Mobile preserves the same object but scales it down inside 32px side gutters.
- The source uses Inter Tight with system-sans fallbacks.
