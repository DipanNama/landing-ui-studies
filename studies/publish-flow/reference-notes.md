# Typefully landing-page reference notes

Source inspected: https://typefully.com/ on September 19, 2026.

## Source observations

- The live page uses Inter and a bright, restrained product-SaaS system: near-black body copy, cool gray dividers, white surfaces, a blue-to-violet accent family, and a near-black trust section.
- The hero is centered and unusually spacious. Its main heading is approximately 44px in the live modal-constrained DOM rules and scales into a much larger desktop display treatment. Supporting copy stays near 500-650px wide.
- The information arc is: concise value proposition and signup choices, a customer-count trust cue, a large product/editor preview, a dense feature-card grid, a free-plan conversion block, then a dark customer/company proof section with another large product interface.
- Product UI is the main visual language. Cards show comments, cross-posting, focused writing, AI assistance, a scheduling calendar, synchronized launch workflows, pixel-perfect previews, and analytics.
- Feature cards use 12-18px radii, 1px cool-gray borders, very light gray fills, and subtle shadows. Their interface miniatures carry more detail than their copy blocks.
- Responsive behavior shifts the three-column feature grid into a horizontally scrollable snap row below 768px. Headline and modal typography step down at small breakpoints. The main content keeps 1rem to 1.5rem side padding.
- Major current source copy includes “Write better content / Grow on social media faster,” “Built for modern teams,” “A better place to write,” “A calendar built for scheduling,” and “Powerful analytics to grow faster.”

## Original-study translation

The recreation is named **Draftly** and uses original positioning, copy, interface labels, avatars, illustrations, brand mark, and UI composition. It retains only the reusable design logic:

- centered conversion hero;
- confident display typography;
- product screenshot as the main proof;
- cool neutral cards with small product mockups;
- blue-violet emphasis;
- a black workflow section near the end;
- compact free-plan CTA;
- responsive product details that simplify rather than shrink.

No source logo, screenshots, avatars, social proof, proprietary media, or other protected branding/assets are included.
