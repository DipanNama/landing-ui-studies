# Reference notes: nan.fyi

Source inspected: https://www.nan.fyi/ (September 19, 2026)

## Visual observations

- A quiet gray paper surface holds a wide, centered shell with thin borders.
- Desktop uses a fixed-width left introduction rail and a larger article feed. The rail becomes a compact top introduction on narrower screens.
- The title is oversized serif type; supporting copy and metadata use a neutral sans and compact mono-like labels.
- Article rows pair authored interactive diagrams with editorial headlines, dates, and short descriptions.
- Rules, dashed separators, small corner details, and restrained gray-on-gray surfaces create a technical notebook character.
- The source is content-first: one promoted course/card followed by a chronological list of long-form interactive articles.

## Original substitutions

This study keeps the layout logic, typography contrast, line work, rhythm, and responsive rail-to-header change. It replaces the source name, author identity, copy, destinations, article subjects, diagrams, logos, and all visual assets. Every diagram is authored locally with HTML/CSS or inline SVG.

## Comparison checklist

- Desktop: 320px sticky rail; spacious reading feed; feature split copy/diagram; article rows align image and text.
- Mobile: rail becomes normal flow; cards stack; touch targets remain usable; no horizontal overflow.
- Surface: warm gray paper, dark ink, fine borders, sparse acid accent.
- Motion: optional, quiet, and limited to a single diagram pulse.
