# Facet Flow - reference notes

Source observed: https://croct.com/ (current landing page), 19 September 2026.

## Observed system

- White SaaS canvas with pale lavender and mint radial glows around the hero.
- Dark navy body ink, slate supporting copy, lavender headline gradient, and mint success accents.
- Desktop hero uses a centered two-line headline around 72px with very tight tracking; mobile recomposes at 32px on the source.
- Thin announcement strip above a spacious navigation with pill actions.
- Hero CTA pair, no-card reassurance, and an oversized interactive product scene below.
- Product demonstration combines a browser-like content canvas with audience/activity cards.
- Later sections use centered headings, rounded feature cards, integration/code proof, customer marks, and a dark final CTA.
- Typography uses Poppins-like rounded display forms and Inter-like body copy.
- Mobile hides full navigation, stacks CTAs, reduces visual density, and lets the hero demonstration focus on one panel.

## Original substitutions in this study

- New product identity: Facet.
- New copy, audiences, examples, client names, code, and CTA language.
- All product scenes, marks, icons, avatars, and diagrams are locally authored CSS.
- System font stack instead of source font files.
- No source logo, screenshots, videos, illustrations, customer logos, copy, or downloaded assets.
